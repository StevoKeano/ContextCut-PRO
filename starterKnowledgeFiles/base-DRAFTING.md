# Document Drafting Best Practices

## Drafting Process
1. **Outline** — Identify all necessary sections
2. **First Draft** — Focus on substance over perfection
3. **Review Cycle** — Check for internal consistency, missing provisions
4. **Authority Check** — Verify all citations and standards
5. **Final Polish** — Grammar, formatting, cross-references

## Universal Principles
- Short sentences, active voice, defined terms
- Clear and precise language — avoid ambiguity
- Logical organization with headings
- Each clause/provision serves a clear purpose
