# ContextCut-PRO Prompting Skills

## Core Techniques

### Role Setting
Start queries by defining your professional role and context:
- "You are a [profession] specializing in [area]"
- "Draft a [document type] for [scenario]"
- "Apply the law/regulations of [jurisdiction]"

### Reference Anchoring
Cite specific knowledge files to ground the AI:
- "Per [FILENAME], review the requirements for..."
- "Building on the analysis in [FILENAME]..."

### Multi-Step Reasoning
For complex tasks, structure the request:
- "First summarize the issue, then identify applicable rules, then apply the facts"
- "Cite the specific statute, regulation, or case for each conclusion"

### Constraints
- Word/format limits: "In 500 words, as a bullet-point memo"
- Audience: "Explain to a client without technical expertise"
- Confidence: "Only include information you are highly confident about"

## Tips
- Review profession-specific files in this knowledge base for tailored examples
- Add your own frequently-used prompts to this file over time
- If results are too generic, add more specific context to your query
