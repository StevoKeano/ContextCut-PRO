# Research Methodology

## Primary Source Hierarchy
*(Order from most to least authoritative for your field)*
1. **Controlling Authority** — Statutes, regulations, binding precedent
2. **Persuasive Authority** — Other jurisdictions, secondary sources
3. **Administrative Guidance** — Agency rulings, advisory opinions
4. **Practice Resources** — Treatises, form books, professional guides

## Research Workflow
1. **Issue Identification** — Pinpoint the precise question
2. **Source Selection** — Identify which authorities govern
3. **Search Execution** — Targeted queries with specific code/rule references
4. **Validation** — Check for amendments, overruling, or updates
5. **Synthesis** — Organize findings by issue; note conflicts and trends
6. **Application** — Apply rules to specific facts; identify gaps
